# Sound, music and editing direction

H3 jointly generates image and native stereo sound. Direct the audiovisual relationship, not independent sound lists.

## Sound layers

Define only story-relevant layers:

- continuous ambience bed with location, time, density and perspective;
- object/wardrobe/body Foley synchronized to visible action;
- discrete effects with source, onset, distance, direction, material and decay;
- dialogue/voice using the six-dimensional contract;
- off-screen sound that causes attention or action;
- silence or reduced bed used deliberately;
- non-diegetic music with narrative function;
- outgoing tail, overlap or bridge.

State what continues across cuts. Do not reset ambience when geography is unchanged. Do not duplicate visible and described sounds in contradictory timing.

## Spatial and perceptual sound

Specify source visibility, on/off screen, near/far, room/outdoor response, stereo position/movement, obstruction, reverberation character and listener perspective only when useful. Preserve consistent acoustic space and voice distance.

## Music

Use music only when it has a function: establish world, shape anticipation, counterpoint action, mark transformation, support montage or resolve a motif. Define instrumentation/timbre, tempo/energy, pulse, density, entry/exit, synchronization policy and whether it is diegetic.

Do not use broad genre labels as the full direction. Do not let music mask dialogue or replace dramatic performance. Use `N/A` when no music is intended.

## Edit rhythm and segmentation

Design rhythm from beat duration, action phase, gaze/reaction, information release and sound. Protect:

- opening orientation/read;
- trigger recognition;
- decisive action/contact;
- reaction/consequence;
- visual and audio settle;
- trim handles when the edit requires them.

Cut on a motivated information, emotion, action, gaze, sound or spatial change. Do not split merely at equal durations. For montage, give each segment one legible image/action idea and define the associative or causal link.

## Transitions

- `cut`: next shot has a complete readable start state.
- `tail_continuity`/exact continuation: preserve the previous visible final state and unfinished action.
- `motion_context`: continue action/camera/audio phase without replay or reset.
- dissolve/fade/match cut: specify the narrative and visual/audio relationship; do not use decoratively.

## Dialogue timing

Reserve ambience lead when requested, natural breath/pauses, listener reaction and a clean tail. Finish exact dialogue and strong transients before the protected end interval. Avoid overlapping voices unless required and tested.

## Failure guards

- Avoid wall-to-wall music and constant loud effects.
- Avoid sound sources with no spatial or visual cause when realism matters.
- Avoid cutting mid-word, mid-contact or before the relationship result is readable unless deliberate.
- Avoid repeating the prior segment's dialogue during continuation.
